## 🪦 Our Story — What Happened at Launch

$UTIL first launched a few years ago as a Cardano utility token, with a community pre-sale meant to fund development. The launch didn't go as planned:

- **Snipers hit the pool** in the first moments after the liquidity pool opened.
- **A badly structured liquidity pool opening** led to an early pump & dump.
- **Some early supporters lost funds** — and, more importantly, the project lost the trust and momentum of its community.
- **Our original Twitter/X account was suspended** without any explanation, cutting off our main community channel.

We own this history. Now we're making it right.

### 🚀 The Restart

The project is back with a **new team, new ideas, and new tools** — stronger liquidity planning, anti-sniper launch tooling, and transparent communication from day one. Same ticker, same Cardano token, sharper plan. See the [Roadmap](https://utilcoin.github.io/utilcoin/roadmap.html).
